# Footer and pages

## Footer

The columns are blocks: **Menu column**, **Text column**, **Newsletter**,
**Social media**, **Follow on Shop**, **Badges** and **Image**. You decide how
many and in what order.

The bottom bar carries the copyright, the store policies, the payment icons and
the country and language selectors. Three switches control it: **Show store
policies**, **Show payment icons** and **Show language and country selectors**.
Leave them on unless you have a reason — a store selling in more than one market
needs the selectors, and payment icons at the foot of the page are what a first
time buyer looks for.

Social links live in **Theme settings → Social media**. Only the networks you
fill in appear.

## Category strip

A section in the footer group, so it shows on every page. Round or square image,
with the category name underneath. Each **Category** block takes a
**Collection**, an **Image** and a **Heading**.

## Mobile bottom bar

Pinned to the bottom, gone from 990px up. Up to five blocks: **Home**,
**Search**, **Account**, **Cart** and a free **Link** with its own icon.

## Floating contact button

Takes any link — WhatsApp (`https://wa.me/55...`), email (`mailto:`) or a
support page. **While the link is empty, the button does not appear.**

## Page templates

Available under **Content → Pages → Template**:

- **page** — an ordinary page, at a comfortable reading width
- **page.contact** — with a contact form
- **page.about** — with image and text, and a benefits strip
- **page.looks** — with a look grid
